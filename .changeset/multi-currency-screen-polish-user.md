# Clearer money screens across currencies

Net worth now leads with one big figure, says how old its exchange rates are, and points out any currency it can't convert yet instead of quietly leaving it out. Account pages show the balance, what's cleared and what the bank says at a glance, the reconcile sheet tells you plainly when you match your statement, and dates read like "Nov 8, 2026" everywhere. Foreign-currency accounts show what they're worth in your own currency right under the balance, the account-type list shows real names like Personal Loan and Solana Wallet, and the screens no longer cramp on small phones or stretch across wide windows.
