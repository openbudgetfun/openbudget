# See your wallet's holdings chain by chain

A wallet on several chains now lists its holdings in one section per chain, each with a stamp, its own total and when it was last read. Staked tokens and unclaimed rewards sit under their coin, a price that is a few days old says so, and a chain that couldn't be read tells you why while still showing its last amounts. Tokens without a price, suspected scams and anything you hide fold into one Hidden group at the bottom, where you can show or hide each one.
