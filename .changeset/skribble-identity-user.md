# A brighter, hand-drawn OpenBudget

OpenBudget has a new look. The icon is now a hot-pink envelope with a gold coin dropping in, and the whole app is brighter: a sunny cream page, bold pink, teal and sunflower accents, and a deep violet ink that stays easy to read in light and dark mode. Outlines, lettering and underlines look drawn by hand. Full envelopes are sealed with wax, the money ready to assign is marked with a highlighter, and new pages slide in behind a stroke of ink. If you have Reduce Motion turned on, everything appears without the animation.
