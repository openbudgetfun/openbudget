# Know what you owe

Set up a tax profile for a budget, then tag envelopes and transactions with tax categories. OpenBudget builds a country tax report with a year switcher, the balance due or refund, the rate bands behind each tax, and the places where your own figures suggest a legal saving. Every report lists what it assumed, where the rates came from, and when they were last checked.
