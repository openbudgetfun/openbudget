# Fill tax years and indirect tax for the Americas and Asia-Pacific

The 101 servable countries of the Americas and Asia-Pacific that task U3 did not cover now carry their individual tax year, default company year end, indirect-tax system, tax authority, graded sources, `verifiedOn`, `recheckBy` and caveats — task U4 of the tax jurisdictions plan, including the non-Gregorian tax years (Nepal's Bikram Sambat, Afghanistan's Solar Hijri) and every rate change since 2022 (Singapore's two GST steps, Indonesia's effective-11% 12%, Malaysia's 8% service tax, India's September 2025 slab reform, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Laos, Vietnam's alternating 8%/10% windows, Fiji's rise-and-cut, Ecuador, Antigua, the Bahamas, Suriname's new VAT and more). The United States now carries all 51 state base sales-tax rates (with the New Mexico, South Dakota and Louisiana changes dated from primary sources) and Canada all 13 provincial GST/HST/PST/QST rates, including Nova Scotia's April 2025 HST cut. Twenty-nine territories with no broad consumption tax of their own record `kind: none` with caveats; the effective-dates test now covers subdivision rows per ISO 3166-2 code. Africa, the Middle East and Central Asia stay null until U5.
