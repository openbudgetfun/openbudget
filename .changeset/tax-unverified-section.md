# See what we could not verify

Your tax report now ends with a "What we could not verify" list: the honest gaps in our research for your country, each saying what needs a second look and none of it hidden in fine print. Figures that come from secondary sources rather than the tax authority itself carry a "From a secondary source" badge on their line, and changes a government has only announced — not yet made law — are labelled as exactly that.
