# Talk to your budget

Ask the assistant how much tax you may owe, where you could cut spending, or whether you are on track for your goals. It reads the plan you are working in, shows what it looked at, and answers in a conversation you can come back to.

When the assistant suggests a change, it waits for you to apply or dismiss it. You can review everything waiting in one place, and every answer carries the same reminder: guidance only, not financial or tax advice.

The included assistant comes with OpenBudget Plus and shows how much of the monthly usage is left. You can also connect your own OpenAI-compatible provider and keep chatting on your own key.
