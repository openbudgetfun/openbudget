# Choose which chains your wallet reads

Open a crypto wallet and tap Wallet settings to rename it, pick the chains it looks at, or disconnect it. Not sure where your assets are? Tap "Look for assets on other chains" and OpenBudget checks and offers anything it finds. If you stop reading a chain that holds money, you're told what will leave your net worth before anything changes.
