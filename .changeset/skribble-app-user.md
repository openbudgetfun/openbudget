# Every screen in the new hand-drawn look

Every screen now wears OpenBudget's new look: hand-drawn outlines, underlined section titles, sticker-style cards and a bright pink Add button. Highlighted figures, like the money ready to assign, are easier to read in dark mode, transaction labels such as "Unassigned" no longer get cut off on smaller phones, and the icons inside text fields, like the envelope beside Email, now sit neatly inside the box instead of on its corner.
