# Your tax profile asks where you are tax resident and whether you are a US person

The tax profile now asks, in every country, whether you are a tax resident there and whether you are a US person, and keeps your answers when you save. Tax-saving ideas wait until you have answered. A count or amount that is not a number is now flagged right under its field, and the form stays in a comfortable column on wide screens.
