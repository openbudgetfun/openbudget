# A new OpenBudget icon

OpenBudget has a new icon: three envelopes on warm paper, each filled to its own level, with the full one sealed in yellow wax. It appears on your home screen, the launch screen, the sign-in screen and the browser tab, with dark and tinted versions on iOS and a themed icon on Android. Under Settings › App Icon you can show it on paper, at night, or in plain ink.
