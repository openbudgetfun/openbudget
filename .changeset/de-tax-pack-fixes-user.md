# German tax reports now show the right numbers

Your German tax reports got a set of corrections: filing deadlines no longer land on weekends, church tax follows your federal state (8% in Bavaria and Baden-Württemberg, 9% everywhere else), couples each get their own worker's allowance, children are credited with whichever is better for you — child benefit or the child allowance — freelancers are no longer charged trade tax, and the commuter and home-office allowances follow the 2025 and 2026 rules. The tax profile asks a few new questions, like your federal state, your children and your commute, so the numbers fit your situation.
