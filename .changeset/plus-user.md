# Subscribe to OpenBudget Plus

OpenBudget Plus is now available in the app: $10 a month for tax reports in
the UK, US, Nigeria and Germany, the built-in assistant with $1 of usage
included every month, and the option to bring your own agent. Subscribe from
the App Store or Google Play on mobile, by card from a browser, or with USDC
from a Solana wallet, then manage or check your plan from Settings.
