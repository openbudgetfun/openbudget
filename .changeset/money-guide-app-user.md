# Meet the Money guide

The assistant is now the Money guide. Its answers carry small numbered marks: tap one to see where a rule or figure comes from, when it was last checked, and whether it comes from a secondary source or describes a rule that is announced but not yet law, then open the source itself. Answers about tax or investments say plainly that they are guidance, not regulated financial advice, and the usage header shows about how many conversations you have left this month.
