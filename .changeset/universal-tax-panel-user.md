# See your tax year and VAT or sales tax rates in any country

Wherever you live, the Taxes screen now shows your tax year, the VAT, GST or sales tax rates with where each one comes from, a link to your tax authority, and a running summary of your income, expenses and tax already paid. Tap "Export tax summary" to download it for your accountant. UK, US, Nigerian and German books keep their full tax report and gain the same download button.
