# Clearer messages when something doesn't load

When your budget, accounts, transactions, payees, rules, recurring items or reports can't load, OpenBudget now says why in plain words (no connection, signed out, or something on our side) instead of showing technical error text. The budget, plan, envelope and transfer screens also gain a Retry button, so you no longer have to leave the screen to try again. Screen readers now announce that you can long-press the month title to jump to a month, and long-press a transaction to flag it.
