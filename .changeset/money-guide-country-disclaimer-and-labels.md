# The Money guide speaks plainly about where it looks and what it is

A Money guide reply about tax or investments now carries the disclaimer for your country, so it says what applies where you live instead of repeating the note under the screen. The small notes about what the guide looked at now say so in plain words, such as "Looked at your tax summary" or "Checked your tax deadlines". The message box now invites questions about money, tax or crypto.
