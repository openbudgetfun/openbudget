# UK tax figures are more accurate

Your UK tax estimates now follow HMRC's rules more closely. Your personal allowance no longer shrinks when pension contributions or Gift Aid bring your income down, student loan repayments match the amounts HMRC collects (and you can tell us about a postgraduate loan on top of your plan), and the 31 January bill shows the real two instalments towards next year instead of a guess. Limited companies pick their actual year end and say how many companies they're connected to, so Corporation Tax lands on the right rates, and big equipment purchases stop above the annual investment allowance until the spending can be claimed. Company owners comparing salary with dividends now see the year's real thresholds, including whether the Employment Allowance applies. The UK tax profile screen asks the new questions under Company and Student loan settings.
