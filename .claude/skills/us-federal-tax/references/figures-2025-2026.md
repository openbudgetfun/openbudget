## SALT cap, IRC 164(b)(6)/(7) (P, https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/164)

| Year      | Cap                | Phase-down threshold (MAGI) |
| --------- | ------------------ | --------------------------- |
| 2025      | 40,000             | 500,000                     |
| 2026      | 40,400             | 505,000                     |
| 2027-2029 | 101% of prior year | 101% of prior year          |
| 2030 on   | 10,000             | n/a                         |

- MFS: cap and threshold halved. Reduced cap = cap minus 30% of (MAGI minus threshold), floor 10,000 (5,000 MFS). MAGI adds back FEIE, Puerto Rico and Form 4563 exclusions. 2025 worksheet: Schedule A instructions https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sca (P).
- 2027 and later: do not compute until the IRS publishes the figure; the 101% rule is statute (P) but rounding is not confirmed (I).

## Other itemized-deduction rules

- Medical above 7.5% of AGI; cash gifts up to 30% of AGI, capital-gain property 20% (P, Schedule A instructions).
- From 2026 (S/P-snip: Pub. 505 and 2026 Form 1040-ES via search summary, CRS R48789 https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48789): non-itemizers deduct up to 1,000 (2,000 MFJ) of cash gifts to qualifying charities (not DAFs or supporting organizations, no noncash, no carryover); itemizers deduct gifts only above 0.5% of AGI with the disallowed part added to the carryover; itemized deductions reduced by 5.4% (2/37) of the lesser of itemized deductions or taxable income over the start of the 37% bracket (640,600 single/HoH, 384,350 MFS).

## Business figures

- QBI thresholds (P): 2025 197,300 single/other and MFS, 394,600 MFJ, phase-in 50,000 / 100,000 (ends 247,300 / 494,600); 2026 201,750 other (201,775 MFS), 403,500 MFJ, phase-in ends 276,750 other / 276,775 MFS / 553,500 MFJ (75,000 / 150,000 ranges). - Section 179 (P): 2025 2,500,000 limit with 4,000,000 phase-out start (OBBBA; pre-OBBBA 1,250,000 / 3,130,000 and SUV cap 31,300 superseded); 2026 2,560,000 limit, 4,090,000 phase-out start, SUV cap 32,000 (Rev. - Bonus depreciation (P, Notice 2026-11 https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-26-11.pdf): permanent 100% for qualified property acquired after 19 January 2025; elect 40% (60% for certain long-production-period property and aircraft) for the first tax year ending after 19 January 2025; elect out by class under 168(k)(7). Qualified production property 100% expensing (168(n)) for construction beginning after 19 January 2025 and before 1 January 2029; domestic research costs immediately deductible from 2025 (Section 174A).
- Standard mileage (P, https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-sets-2026-business-standard-mileage-rate-at-725-cents-per-mile-up-25-cents): 2026 business 72.5 cents, medical and moving 20.5, charitable 14. 2025 business 70, medical and moving 21, charitable 14 (I, derived from the 2026 notice; Schedule E instructions confirm 70 cents for 2025, https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040se). The charitable rate is statutory.
- Excess business loss limit permanent; 2025 threshold 626,000 joint / 313,000 others (P). 2026 threshold not found.
- FEIE 130,000 (2025), 132,900 (2026); both-qualifying couple up to 260,000 for 2025 (P). Housing limit 30% of FEIE: 39,000 (2025), 39,870 (2026) (P/I); location caps in Notice 2026-25 (https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-26-25.pdf), for example Hong Kong 114,300, London 68,600, Singapore 86,700 (P-snip). - Section 163(j): EBITDA-based ATI for years after 2024; applies above 31 million average gross receipts (P).
- Employer meals largely nondeductible from 2026 (P); business meals 50% on Schedule C line 24b (80% for DOT-regulated workers).
