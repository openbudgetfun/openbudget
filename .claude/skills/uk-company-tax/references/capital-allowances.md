## Hybrid rate

Hybrid WDA rate = `(daysBefore x oldRate + daysAfter x newRate) / apDays`. [I], recomputed and matched a secondary figure (about 14.99% for a 31 December 2026 year end, https://www.menzies.co.uk/autumn-budget-2025-whats-changed-for-capital-allowances/, S).

Apply the hybrid rate to the whole pool for that AP. Use exact integer day counts and rational arithmetic.

## Announced, not enacted

Budget 2026 policy paper (published 29 October 2026), legislation in Finance Bill 2026-27 (amends CAA 2001 s52(3) and s56(1)): [P for the announcement, not enacted]
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/reduce-the-main-rate-writing-down-allowance-and-increase-the-main-rate-first-year-allowance/capital-allowances-reduce-the-main-rate-writing-down-allowance-and-increase-the-main-rate-first-year-allowance

- Main pool WDA 14% to 12% for chargeable periods beginning on or after 1 April 2027 (CT). Hybrid rate for a period that begins before and ends on or after the change date.
- Main-rate FYA 40% to 50% for expenditure from 1 January 2027.
- Special rate WDA stays 6%. No change to AIA or full expensing.
- Example: AP 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2027 begins before and ends after 1 April 2027, so the hybrid applies: `(90 x 14% + 275 x 12%) / 365 = 12.4932%`. Confirm the day-count wording against the Finance Act text before use. [I]

The research notes mix two Budget dates (28 October in one set of notes, policy paper 29 October in another). Treat the policy paper date as authoritative and re-check.

## Implementation notes

- Model per-AP pools: main pool, special rate pool, single-asset pools, with balancing allowances and charges on disposal. Each addition has a date, cost, type (plant, car with CO2, EV charge point), and allowance route.
- Order of relief per AP: full expensing or 50% FYA on qualifying new spend, AIA on the rest (and on second-hand), FYAs, then WDA on the pool after additions. AIA, FYA and WDA do not stack on the same pound.
- The repo today treats everything in `gb.co.capital_allowances` as AIA at 100% with no £1m cap, no pools, no WDA, no balancing charges, no cars (see the assumptions in `gb_tax_rules.dart`). Add an AIA cap and a warning at minimum.
- Not sourced: whether the 40% FYA combines with AIA on the same asset, full expensing special-rate details, the "expenditure incurred" timing rules.
