# Business taxes: EÜR vs Bilanz, AfA, Gewerbesteuer, §35, GmbH/UG

Quality tags as in `income-tax.md`. Research date 5 Nov 2026.

## EÜR vs Bilanzierung

- §141 AO thresholds from 2024: 800,000 turnover or 80,000 profit (were 600,000 / 60,000) for Gewerbetreibende and Land- und Forstwirte. Either limit triggers the duty, but only after the Finanzamt notifies. Freiberufler are exempt. §241a HGB exempts Einzelkaufleute at 800,000 / 80,000 in two consecutive years. No change for 2026. **Secondary.** The legal vehicle is not pinned down (one source credits the Wachstumschancengesetz of 27 Mar 2024, none mentions BEG IV). [blind-partner.de](https://www.blind-partner.de/neue-buchfuehrungsgrenzen-nach-%C2%A7-141-ao-ab-2024/), [steuerschroeder.de](https://www.steuerschroeder.de/Buchfuehrungspflicht.html), [finom.co](https://finom.co/de-de/blog/buchfuehrungspflicht/).
- GmbH/UG are Formkaufleute: always double-entry; no EÜR route (inferred from the §241a HGB scope). They still deliver EÜR data to Z75 (2025) / Z76 (2026) with Anlage GK completing the Einkünfte.
- Freiberufler status (Katalogberufe) not researched. The pack has no field for Einkunftsart; Anlage S (freiberuflich, Kz 100/300) versus Anlage G (gewerblich, Kz 10/11) must come from the user.

## Gewerbesteuer

- Freibetrag 24,500 for natural persons and partnerships (§11 Abs. 1 GewStG); none for GmbH/UG. Messzahl 3.5%. Gewerbeertrag floored to 100 before the Freibetrag. Hebesatz set by the municipality, minimum 200%. Hinzurechnung: 25% of Finanzierungsaufwendungen above a 200,000 allowance. **Secondary.** [bsteuern.com](https://www.bsteuern.com/blog/gewerbesteuer-freibetrag), [selbststaendig.de](https://www.selbststaendig.de/wissen/gewerbesteuer-messbetrag), [rechnercheck.de](https://rechnercheck.de/rechner/gewerbesteuer/). The statute (§§7 to 9 Kürzungen and Hinzurechnungen) was not read.
- Freiberufler owe none.
- Prepayments (§19 GewStG, primary): 15 Feb, 15 May, 15 Aug, 15 Nov, one quarter of the last assessed tax each, only if at least 50. [§19](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewstg/__19.html).
- Open question: whether the Messbetrag is floored to full euros. The research example keeps cents (1,242.50) and notes that practice floors it, unsourced. Read §11 GewStG before relying on either.
- Scheduled change flagged from 2027: minimum Hebesatz 280% (one secondary source, unconfirmed).
- No free official Hebesatz dataset was identified. Make Hebesatz a user input with a stated 400% default.

## §35 EStG credit

Credit factor 4.0 since 2020 (was 3.8). Credit = min(4.0 × Messbetrag, GewSt actually paid, income tax attributable to the commercial income). Applies to sole traders and Mitunternehmer, not to GmbH/UG/AG. **Secondary.** [steuerschroeder.de](https://www.steuerschroeder.de/Steuerrechner/Gewerbesteueranrechnung.html), [gewerbe-anmelden.info](https://www.gewerbe-anmelden.info/steuern/gewerbesteuer). Forms: Anlage G 2025 Z51 to Z57 (Kz 64/65 Messbetrag, 66/67 GewSt payable). The attribution of income tax to commercial income is "tax × commercial income / total positive income" in the current pack (inferred; statute not read).

## Körperschaftsteuer and GmbH/UG

- KSt (§23 Abs. 1 KStG) 15% through 2027. From 2028: 14% (2028), 13% (2029), 12% (2030), 11% (2031), 10% from 2032. Promulgated BGBl. 2025 I Nr. 161 on 18 Jul 2025, in force 19 Jul 2025. **Primary-leaning** (BMF Monatsbericht and Bundestag Drs. 21/323 cited): [BMF Monatsbericht Aug 2025](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Monatsberichte/Ausgabe/2025/08/Inhalte/Kapitel-2-Fokus/investitionssofortprogramm-deutschland.html), [Bundestag Drs. 21/323](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/003/2100323.pdf), [Haufe](https://www.haufe.de/steuern/gesetzgebung-politik/gesetz-fuer-ein-steuerliches-investitionssofortprogramm_168_650104.html), [advisio.team](https://www.advisio.team/blog-aktuelles/artikel/senkung-der-koerperschaftsteuersteuer-ab-2028). Follow-up legislation is still needed for the Kapitalertragsteuer procedure and non-resident withholding.
- Soli on KSt: 5.5%, no Freigrenze.
- Forms: KSt 1 is a cover form with no Besteuerungsgrundlagen; income in Vordruck GK (Z11 Jahresüberschuss/-fehlbetrag), taxable income in Anlage ZVE (Z32 is "Einkommen im Sinne des §9 Abs. 2 Satz 1 KStG"), loss carry-forwards in Anlage Verluste; Anlage Zinsschranke, Z, AE for special cases. Returns are electronic only (§31 Abs. 1a KStG). GewSt 1 A is a separate return. E-Bilanz XBRL transmission applies. KSt/GewSt Kz are not retrieved ([Haufe Leitfaden](https://www.haufe.de/id/beitrag/leitfaden-2025-vordruck-kst-1-HI16941392.html), [ELSTER Hilfe KSt 2025](https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_kst_2025), [§31 KStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kstg_1977/__31.html)). A search hit "K 1 - Körperschaftsteuererklärung 2025" with Kz 9040, 9310 is the Austrian form: ignore.
- KSt prepayments for calendar-year corporations: 10 Mar, 10 Jun, 10 Sep, 10 Dec (inferred from §31 Abs. 1 KStG and §37 EStG).
- Not researched: Jahresabschluss size classes (§267, §267a HGB), E-Bilanz taxonomy version, publication in the Unternehmensregister (12 months after year-end per background), Geschäftsführergehalt benchmarks.
- Gesellschafter-Geschäftsführer with more than 50% or a blocking minority is generally not subject to social insurance (statute knowledge, not researched; verify before building payroll).

## vGA and dividends (primary statute)

- §8 Abs. 3 KStG: whether income is distributed is irrelevant for determining income; vGA and Genussrecht distributions do not reduce income; verdeckte Einlagen do not increase income (but do if they reduced the shareholder's income). [§8 KStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kstg_1977/__8.html).
- The arm's-length criteria (Fremdvergleich, Angemessenheit, Rückwirkungsverbot, formal clauses) are case law and BMF letters and were not researched. Any payment above an arm's-length level is an add-back in Anlage GK and dividend for the shareholder.
- Dividends: Abgeltungsteuer 25% plus Soli (withheld by the GmbH), or Teileinkünfteverfahren; see `income-tax.md`.
