## Umsatzsteuer

### Rates

19% (§12 Abs. 1) and 7% (§12 Abs. 2, Anlage 2 supplies). Not re-fetched in the research; statute knowledge. The restaurant-food rate change for 2026 was not researched.

### Kleinunternehmer (§19 UStG) from 1 Jan 2025

- Prior-year Gesamtumsatz at most 25,000 and current-year at most 100,000 (was 22,000 / 50,000). The turnover that crosses 100,000 is already taxed at the regular rate. In the founding year the 25,000 limit applies. The waiver binds at least five calendar years and must be declared by the end of February of the second following year. The invoice must state the exemption (for example "steuerfreier Kleinunternehmer nach § 19 UStG"). A simplified invoice under the new §34a UStDV is allowed. Kleinunternehmer need not issue e-invoices but must be able to receive them. Kleinunternehmer file no UStVA except for §13b tax. [BMF letter 18 Mar 2025](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-03-18-sonderregelung-kleinunternehmer.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3) (title seen), [finanz.de](https://www.finanz.de/business/kleinunternehmerregelung/), [taxfix.de](https://taxfix.de/ratgeber/selbststaendige/kleinunternehmergrenze/), [steuertipps.de](https://www.steuertipps.de/selbststaendigkeit/wichtige-aenderungen-fuer-kleinunternehmer-ab-2025). The thresholds are confirmed by the 2025 annual USt form instructions (primary content, third-party copy: [taxpool.net](https://umsatzsteuer-erklaerung.taxpool.net/Umsatzsteuererkl%C3%A4rung-2025.pdf)).
- An EU-resident small business may use the exemption in Germany if German prior-year turnover is at most 25,000, EU-wide at most 100,000 and a Kleinunternehmer-Identifikationsnummer was issued by its home state.
- Forms: UStVA Z12 Kz 70 (date of change or crossing), Kz 48 for tax-free §19 Abs. 1 turnover in a regular filing; annual return Kz 136 (date), Kz 236 (§19 turnover; Z48 in 2025, Z53 in 2026); EÜR Kz 111 (Z12), with Kz 112 for the taxable part and Kz 119 for non-taxable amounts when the 100,000 line is crossed (form text, not a worked rule).
- Test cases: prior year 24,999 and current forecast under 100,000: exempt. Prior year 25,001: regular. Current year reaches 100,001: the invoice that crosses carries VAT.

### Voranmeldung periods (§18 UStG, primary [§18](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__18.html))

- Due and payable on the 10th day after the period. The regular period is the quarter. Month if the prior-year tax was above 9,000. If the prior-year tax was at most 2,000 (threshold was 1,000 until 2024) the Finanzamt can exempt the filer, leaving only the annual return. In the founding year and the following year the month applies (as written in the research; the researcher did not confirm a temporary suspension of this rule for founders and statute knowledge says there may be one). Annual return: Abschlusszahlung due one month after filing.
- Dauerfristverlängerung (USt 1 H) extends filing and payment by one month. Monthly filers pay a Sondervorauszahlung of 1/11 of the prior year's remaining prepayments (plus the previous Sondervorauszahlung), recalculated yearly and offset in the December return (Kz 39 on UStVA, Kz 38 on the application). Quarterly filers pay none. Secondary for the 2025/2026 thresholds: [steuerschroeder.de](https://www.steuerschroeder.de/Steuerrechner/Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldungen.html), [papierkram.de](https://www.papierkram.de/wissen/umsatzsteuer/verfahren/voranmeldung-und-dauerfristverlaengerung/), [pandotax.de](https://pandotax.de/rechtliches/umsatzsteuervoranmeldung-grenzen-2026/).
- UStVA is submitted electronically and authenticated (§18 Abs. 1 UStG with §48 UStDV).

### §13b reverse charge (primary [§13b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__13b.html))

Cases: (1) EU services under §3a Abs. 2 by an EU-based business; Abs. 2 Nr. 1 supplies by a foreign-established business, Nr. 2 Sicherungsübereignung outside insolvency, Nr. 3 supplies covered by Grunderwerbsteuer, Nr. 4 Bauleistungen, Nr. 5 gas and electricity, Nr. 6 emission certificates, Nr. 7 Anlage-3 goods (scrap and similar), Nr. 8 Gebäudereinigung, Nr. 9 gold of at least 325/1000, Nr. 10 mobile phones, tablets, consoles and integrated circuits (5,000 minimum per transaction), Nr. 11 Anlage-4 goods (5,000), Nr. 12 Telekommunikationsleistungen. The recipient owes the tax if an Unternehmer or juristische Person; for Bauleistungen and Gebäudereinigung only if they regularly supply such services (Finanzamt certificate, valid three years); for Telekommunikation only if the main activity is reselling. A Kleinunternehmer recipient still owes §13b tax. Anlage 3 and 4 goods lists were not read. Reporting: buyer UStVA Z30 Kz 46/47, Z31 Kz 73/74, Z32 Kz 84/85 and Vorsteuer Z41 Kz 67; seller Z34 Kz 60.

### Ist-Versteuerung (§20 UStG, primary [§20](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__20.html))

On application the Finanzamt may allow tax on received payments if the prior-year Gesamtumsatz (§19 Abs. 2) was at most 800,000, or the business is exempt from bookkeeping under §148 AO, or for Freiberufler turnover (§18 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 EStG). Model it as an approved status per period, not an automatic rule at 800,000. Annual form Kz 133: 1 = vereinbarte Entgelte (§16), 2 = vereinnahmte (§20), 3 = partly.

### §19a UStG (primary [§19a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__19a.html))

A German business applies to the BZSt for a Kleinunternehmer-Identifikationsnummer to use the exemption in other EU states. Conditions: EU-wide prior-year turnover at most 100,000 and not expected above it this year, the other state's conditions met, no registration there. Quarterly Umsatzmeldung within one month after quarter end; crossing 100,000 needs a notification within 15 working days and ends participation. The "EX" suffix of the number was not confirmed. OSS and Zusammenfassende Meldung were not researched.

## E-invoicing (secondary)

- 1 Jan 2025: receiving structured e-invoices (EN 16931: XRechnung, ZUGFeRD/Factur-X) mandatory.
- Through 31 Dec 2026: paper and plain PDF remain valid for issuing.
- From 1 Jan 2027: businesses with 2026 turnover above 800,000 must issue structured e-invoices. Others may use paper or other electronic invoices (with recipient consent) through 31 Dec 2027.
- From 1 Jan 2028: all affected businesses, Kleinunternehmer excepted.
- BMF letter of 15 Oct 2025 (III C 2 - S 7287-a/00019/007/243) adds validation duties and distinguishes format, business-rule and content errors. One article dated it 15 Oct 2026, likely a typo.
- Small-amount invoices up to 250: the source phrasing ("may be e-invoices without consent") is ambiguous; statute knowledge says Kleinbetragsrechnungen up to 250 gross are outside the duty (§33 UStDV). Verify.
- Sources: [rnhs.de](https://www.rnhs.de/wissen/elektronische-rechnungen), [ecovis-kso.com](https://ecovis-kso.com/blog/e-rechnung-2026-ergaenzende-hinweise/), [bakertilly.de](https://www.bakertilly.de/beitrag/e-rechnung-zweites-bmf-schreiben-sorgt-fuer-mehr-klarheit), [IHK München](https://www.ihk-muenchen.de/ratgeber/steuern/steuerliche-sonderthemen/elektronische-rechnungen/), [ad-hoc-news.de](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/wirtschaft/e-rechnung-bmf-haelt-an-pflicht-ab-2027-fuer-firmen-ueber-800-000-euro-fest/70356898).
- Product impact (inferred): the invoicing module must emit structured output for users with 2026 turnover above 800,000 from 1 Jan 2027 and for everyone from 1 Jan 2028 (except Kleinunternehmer). Archive the XML original (GoBD below).

### Minijob (secondary)

2026 limit 603 per month (7,236 per year) = 13.90 × 130 / 3 = 602.33 rounded up; 2025 limit 556 (12.82 × 130 / 3 = 555.53). Minimum wage 13.90 from 1 Jan 2026 (12.82 before). Midijob zone 603.01 to 2,000. Employer flat-rate contributions for commercial employers at most 31.17% (about 187.96 at 603). A single source reports 2027 values (minimum wage 14.60, Minijob limit 633, Minijob-Zentrale notice of 7 Oct 2026); unverified at origin. ### Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung (primary, BMF Muster 2026)

Period by prior-year Lohnsteuer: above 5,000 monthly; above 1,080 up to 5,000 quarterly; 1,080 or less annual. Annualised for a business not existing the full prior year; first full month annualised for a new business. Filing and payment on the 10th after the period. Säumniszuschlag 1% of the arrears floored to 50, per started month. Amounts below 1 in total are not collected or refunded. [BMF Muster Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung 2026](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Lohnsteuer/2025-08-14-Muster-Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung-2026.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=6). The first research note's 2,000 / 8,000 thresholds are wrong and must not be used. Kz table in `elster-ust-lst.md`.

Not researched: Lohnsteuer calculation (BMF PAP 2025/2026), Pflegeversicherung child discounts, Umlagen U1/U2/U3 percentages.

### Weekend roll-forward (§108 Abs. 3 AO)

A Frist that ends on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday ends on the next working day. The research did not fetch §108 AO; the rule is confirmed by the day-of-week arithmetic below and by the secondary pages that already roll VZ 2025 to 1 Mar 2027. Public holidays are regional: the deadline function needs a holiday calendar by Land. Statute knowledge, unverified: §108 moves filing deadlines, not the due date (Fälligkeit) itself, and payment is judged against the three-day Schonfrist of §240 AO. Check before building payment reminders.

### Verspätungszuschlag (§152 AO, primary [§152](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__152.html))

- Mandatory (Abs. 2) if the annual return is not filed within 14 months after the end of the calendar year (§152 Abs. 2 Nr. 1); Nr. 2 cases (those of §149 Abs. 2 Satz 2) have a longer period (19 months in the research text; the EGAO table gives 22 months for VZ 2023, so the two figures are for different cases or years).
- Amount (Abs. 5): 0.25% of the assessed tax (less prepayments and withholding) per started month, at least 25 per started month for annual returns (10 per month in general cases), cap 25,000 (Abs. 10).
- Abs. 3: not imposed where the tax is zero or negative or covered by prepayments, or where the deadline was extended (§109). Abs. 8: Voranmeldungen are excluded from the fixed formula; the surcharge there depends on duration, frequency and amount.
- Transition months (§152 Abs. 2 Nr. 1): 2023: 17; 2024: 16. A VZ 2024 return is mandatory-surcharge-relevant after 16 months, so end of April 2026.
- Conflict resolved: the 22-month figure in a Volksbank page is a transition figure, not the baseline.
- As of 5 Nov 2026 a late VZ 2025 self-filer is inside the 14-month window to end of Feb 2027: surcharge discretionary until then. Whether §108 also moves the 14-month date is not checked.
- Säumniszuschlag for late payment is 1% per started month of the arrears (LStA form text).
- §233a AO interest (Nachzahlungszinsen) was not researched.

## ELSTER and ERiC (out of scope for OpenBudget)

- Mein ELSTER has no general import for third-party return files; the only support is a JustAnswer reply (weak). Third-party software transmits through ERiC; the ElsterFormular route is discontinued. [JustAnswer](https://www.justanswer.de/finanzamt/t74ul-kann-man-die-versendeten-steuererkl-rungen-die-mit-wiso.html), [Finanztip](https://www.finanztip.de/elster/), [ElsterFormular Handbuch](https://download.elster.de/download/anleitung/Handbuch_ElsterFormular.pdf).
- ERiC is free for registered software vendors. The Bavarian tax office (LfSt) checks that you build tax software and issues a Hersteller-ID. Licence terms (Release 41): follow the documentation, check for new versions, bear all integration, update and support costs; the LfSt can prohibit use if third-party IP is affected. Authenticated submission needs the taxpayer's Mein ELSTER certificate. Available for Windows, Linux, macOS, AIX. A third-party summary says the terms exclude free (open-source) software; the full wording on open source and SaaS/server-side use was not read. [ERiC Lizenzvertrag Release 41 (Scopevisio copy)](https://help.scopevisio.com/en/articles/467538-licence-agreement-eric-release-41), [ELSTER Entwickler](https://www.elster.de/elsterweb/infoseite/entwickler), [Wikipedia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elster_(Software)), [ERiC Entwicklerhandbuch](https://instantview.org/data/CyberEnterprise/ERiC/ERiC-Entwicklerhandbuch.pdf).
- Some vendors submit without the user's own certificate (Check24 Steuer named). [t-online.de](https://www.t-online.de/finanzen/geld-vorsorge/steuern/id_89083634/steuererklaerung-ohne-elster-welche-alternativen-gibt-es-.html), [Finanztip](https://www.finanztip.de/steuersoftware/).
- Not researched: whether ERiC has a validate-only mode, and whether Plausibilitätsprüfung works without registration.
- Decision: OpenBudget produces a Kz checklist and PDF only. Direct filing would add a heavy security burden (holding a .pfx and PIN), conflicts with the project's privacy stance, and the open-source licence restriction would matter if the code base is opened.
