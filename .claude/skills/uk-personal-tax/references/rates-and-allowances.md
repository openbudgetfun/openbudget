| Item                            | 2025/26 | 2026/27 | Tag |
| ------------------------------- | ------- | ------- | --- |
| Class 4 Lower Profits Limit     | 12,570  | 12,570  | P   |
| Class 4 Upper Profits Limit     | 50,270  | 50,270  | P   |

- Compulsory Class 2 was abolished from 6 April 2024. Profits at or above the Small Profits Threshold count as paid; below it nothing is due but voluntary Class 2 can be paid. Software only needs the voluntary flag (SA103F box 100 / SA103S box 36) and the deadline: voluntary Class 2 for 2025/26 cannot be paid through Self Assessment after 31 January 2027.
- From 6 April 2026 voluntary Class 2 cannot be paid for periods abroad; only Class 3 (10 continuous years in the UK or 10 qualifying years).
- Class 4 exemption (SA103F box 101 / SA103S box 37): State Pension age reached on or before 6 April 2025, under 16 on 6 April 2025, or not UK resident in 2025/26.
- Class 4 base is taxable profits after loss relief plus transition profits, adjusted by SA103F box 102. It is not reduced by pension contributions or Gift Aid. Class 4 is included in payments on account.
- LPL and UPL are held to April 2031.
- Class 4 for people who also have employment (Class 1 interaction, annual maximum) was not researched.
- Sources: [NIC rates and allowances](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-allowances-national-insurance-contributions), [Self-employed NI rates](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-national-insurance-rates), [GOV.UK summary of Class 2 abolition](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/changes-to-national-insurance-contributions-from-6-january-2024/a-reduction-in-the-main-rates-of-primary-class-1-and-class-4-national-insurance-contributions-and-the-removal-of-the-requirement-to-pay-class-2-nation), [Voluntary NI abroad from 6 April 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/voluntary-national-insurance-contributions-for-periods-abroad-from-6-april-2026/voluntary-national-insurance-contributions-abroad-from-6-april-2026), [SA103F notes](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/69c26565cfa346b9d4704b35/SA103F_Notes_2026.pdf).

## 7. Pensions, Gift Aid, adjusted net income

- Annual allowance 60,000 (2025/26, 2026/27, 2027/28); threshold income 200,000; adjusted income 260,000; minimum tapered allowance 10,000; MPAA 10,000; taper 1 per 2 above 260,000 (10,000 reached at 360,000); carry forward unused allowance from the previous 3 tax years, current year first then earliest year first; no carry forward onto MPAA. Lump sum allowance 268,275; lump sum and death benefit allowance 1,073,100. **P**: [Pension schemes rates](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-pension-schemes/pension-schemes-rates), [tapered annual allowance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/pension-schemes-work-out-your-tapered-annual-allowance).
- Relief on member contributions is limited to the higher of 100% of UK relevant earnings and 3,600. **P**.
- Relief at source (provider adds 20%): enter the **gross** amount in SA100 TR 4 box 1 (net divided by 0.8). Net-pay arrangements (deducted from pay before tax) are not entered. The gross amount extends the basic-rate and higher-rate bands for the taxpayer and reduces adjusted net income. Annual allowance excess goes on SA101 page Ai 4 boxes 10 to 12. **P** for the box rules ([SA150 notes](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a9ea8a5df4246cf45e469e0/SA150_Notes_2026.pdf)); band extension is stated in the SA110 notes; the exact adjusted-net-income definition was not quoted from legislation (**I**).
- Gift Aid: SA100 TR 4 boxes 5 to 10; no relief for gifts to non-UK charities after 5 April 2024; payroll giving excluded. Whether box 5 expects the net or the grossed-up amount was **not verified** against the form wording: read the SA150 box 5 note before showing a value.
- Salary-sacrifice pension contributions above 2,000 a year attract NIC from 6 April 2029 (Budget 2025, employer side). **P**.

## 8. High Income Child Benefit Charge (HICBC)

- Threshold 60,000 adjusted net income (from 2024/25). Charge is 1% of the Child Benefit for every 200 of adjusted net income over 60,000; all repaid at 80,000. The charge falls on the higher earner of a couple where either receives Child Benefit. **P**: [GOV.UK Child Benefit tax charge](https://www.gov.uk/child-benefit-tax-charge).
- SA110 working sheet: J3 = ANI minus 60,000; J4 = J3 x 0.5%; J6 = Child Benefit x 1%; J7 = J4 x J6; J8 = lower of Child Benefit and J7. No explicit rounding of J4 to a whole 200 step is shown; whether HMRC rounds the percentage down is **not confirmed**. **P** for the sheet, gap for rounding.
- Weekly Child Benefit: 2026/27 eldest 27.05, other 17.90 (**P**, Budget 2026 Annex A). Full weeks in the SA100 box count Mondays in the tax year: 52 in 2025/26 (7 April 2025 to 30 March 2026), 53 in 2026/27 (**I**, counted).
- Winter Fuel Payment charge: total income over 35,000 and no qualifying benefit; SA100 TR 5 (Winter Fuel section) box 1.

## 12. Corrections applied (payroll specification note overrides)

1. Plan 4 2025/26 threshold is 32,745. The SA110 notes (page TCSN 49) print 31,395, which is the 2024/25 figure and stale.
2. The SA110 working sheet K40 says "TR 5 box 13". The 2025/26 SA100 has Student Loan deducted at TR 5 (Student Loan section) **box 2**.
3. Payments-on-account reduction boxes 10, 11 and 17 are on the **SA110 Tax calculation summary**, not SA100 TR 6 (where boxes 10 and 11 are nominee boxes).
