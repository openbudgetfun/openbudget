# UK levers (2025/26 and 2026/27)

Grades: **[P]** primary, **[S]** secondary, **[I]** inferred or computed, **[B]** background knowledge not fetched (do not present as settled). England, Wales and Northern Ireland unless stated. Frozen thresholds assumed: personal allowance £12,570, basic-rate band £37,700, higher-rate threshold £50,270, allowance taper £1 per £2 over £100,000 and lost at £125,140. Thresholds frozen to 5 April 2031 [S] (https://www.rathbones.com/en-gb/wealth-management/knowledge-and-insight/autumn-budget-2026-what-it-means-for-you).

## Not to build

- Anything that picks an investment, fund, provider or withdrawal rate.
- Residence-based arrangements (see `cross-border.md`).
- Settlements-style income shifting (see `anti-avoidance-never-suggest.md`).
