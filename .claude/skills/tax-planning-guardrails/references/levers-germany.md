# Germany levers (calendar 2025 and 2026)

Grades: **[P]** primary, **[S]** secondary, **[I]** computed, **[B]** background not fetched. No German primary source for amounts (Bundesfinanzministerium, EStG on gesetze-im-internet.de) was fetched in this research; only StBerG §§5 and 6 were. The repo cites the EStG sections directly, which is better evidence than these notes; reconcile before changing a figure.

Product rule: general explanation, calculators with stated assumptions, and links to ELSTER, a Lohnsteuerhilfeverein or a Steuerberater. No "Handlungsempfehlung für Ihren Einzelfall". GmbH and holding comparisons are signpost-only.

## Hazards for opportunity code

- A GmbH shareholder-director Gehalt-versus-Ausschüttung comparison is Einzelfall tax advice and is exposed to §5 StBerG and verdeckte Gewinnausschüttung risk (see `anti-avoidance-never-suggest.md`). The repo has one (`de.geschaeftsfuehrer_vergleich`); treat it as signpost-only until counsel clears it.
- Copy must say general rule plus the user's recorded figures, never a personal recommendation.
- Soli, Kirchensteuer and the deductible percentage must be explicit inputs, not silent assumptions.
