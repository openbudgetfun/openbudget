## Personal income tax

Bands from 1 January 2026: first ₦800,000 at 0%; next ₦2.2m at 15%; next ₦9m at 18%; next ₦13m at 21%; next ₦25m at 23%; above ₦50m at 25%. Sources (as summarised in search results): https://www.safeguardglobal.com/resources/blog/nigeria-tax-act-2025/ , https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/nigeria-tax-act-2025-has-been-signed-highlights , https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/flash-alert-2025-168.html
